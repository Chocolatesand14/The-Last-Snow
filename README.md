# ❄️ The Last Snow

*Some mysteries must be solved before they melt away.*

## About the Game

The Last Snow is a story-driven 2D pixel art adventure 
set in a mysterious winter world.

A little snowman wakes up alone in a snowy forest.
He doesn't know who he is, who built him, or why
he has come to life.

With no memories of his past, he sets out on a journey 
to uncover the truth behind his existence.

But winter is slowly coming to an end, and so is his time.

Will he discover who he is before he melts away?

## Planned Features

- Top-down exploration
- Original pixel art characters
- Character movement and animations
- Temperature and melting mechanics
- Story progression and hidden memories
- Interactive environments
- Inventory system
- NPC interactions and dialogue
- Environmental puzzles
- Save and load functionality
